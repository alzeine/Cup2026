About this tracker

Your personal
World Cup
schedule

A simple way to follow the 2026 FIFA World Cup — built for fans who care about specific teams and want to know exactly when to tune in, without wading through all 104 matches.

⭐
Pick your teams
Open the team menu and tap any team you want to follow. You can select as many teams as you like. Once selected, switch to the "My Teams" view and see only those matches, cutting out all the noise.
🕐
All times in CEST — Stockholm time
The World Cup spans three host countries and several time zones. Every kick-off time on this tracker is converted to Central European Summer Time (UTC+2), so if you're based in Europe you always know exactly when to sit down in front of the TV. No mental arithmetic required.
🍪
Your favourites are remembered
Your selected teams are saved in a cookie in your browser. Close the tab, shut down your laptop, come back a week later — your team list will still be there. Nothing is stored on a server; it lives entirely on your own device.
📡
Live data from openfootball
All match data — fixtures, kick-off times, venues, and results — is fetched directly from the open-source openfootball project on GitHub. Scores refresh automatically every 5 minutes, and you can also hit the refresh button any time to pull the latest.
